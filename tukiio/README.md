# Tukiio — Research Dossier

Everything we know about **Tukiio** (https://tukiio.com), gathered 27 Oct 2026 and organised for designing the new landing page with the design language in [../DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

## TL;DR

- **Tukiio** = "tukio" (Swahili for *event*). It is Tanzania's all-in-one **event ticketing and management platform**, made by **Dephics Company Limited** (Dar es Salaam). It grew out of **TiME Tickets** (mobile-money QR tickets, founded 2013, publicly "since 2015").
- It does **much more than ticketing**: registration, check-in, live chat/Q&A/polls, **voting & awards**, marathons (bibs, races), memberships, raffles, donations, cashless cards, white-label portals. It also supplies **paper QR tickets (from TZS 500/pc), wristbands, badges, scanners and displays**.
- It sells **everywhere**: web, apps, **USSD/feature phone**, SMS, agents and paper. Payments run on **mobile money**, **TanQR** and **cards (DPO)**.
- **Pricing:** free events are free forever; **7%** commission on paid tickets; **payouts within 24h**; custom packages.
- **Brand:** yellow `#FFC50B` + black, lowercase `tukiio` wordmark whose **"ii" are two people with yellow dots**, and a two-dot bounce loader. Font today: Product Sans (proprietary, so it needs replacing).
- **Current site** is an event marketplace feed with **no organiser pitch**, weak SEO and no social proof. That's the big opportunity.
- **Design fit:** Tukiio's palette has the same structure as our **LIME** variant (highlighter + ink + white). The proposal is a **"GOLD"** variant: LIME structure + EMBER warmth + Tukiio assets ([06](06-design-language-fit.md)).
