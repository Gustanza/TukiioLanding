1. **Create your event.** Add ticket types, prices and dates in minutes. Free events are free, forever.

3. **Check in & get paid.** Scan guests at the door and receive your money within 24 hours.

## FAQ drafts

- **How much does Tukiio cost?** Free events are free forever. For paid events we charge 7% of the ticket price, which covers payment and transfer fees. Custom packages are available for large events.
- **When do I get my money?** Funds are processed to your preferred account within 24 hours of your request.
- **How can my audience pay?** Mobile money, TanQR, USSD and debit/credit card.† Fans can also buy from a feature phone without internet.
- **Can you print tickets, wristbands and badges?** Yes. QR-coded, verifiable paper tickets start from TZS 500 per piece, and we also supply wristbands, badges, scanners and displays.
- **How do I check guests in?** Download the Tukiio Organizer app, add your team, and scan tickets at the entrance. Desk registration is supported too.
- **Can attendees buy for a group?** Yes. Group and multiple-ticket purchases are supported, including group registration for races.
- **Do you support voting and awards?** Yes. Run public voting campaigns with phone verification via SMS or WhatsApp.
- **Is Tukiio available in Kiswahili?** Yes, the platform supports English and Kiswahili.
- **Can I have my own branded portal?** Yes. Institutions can run a white-label Tukiio portal with their own branding.
