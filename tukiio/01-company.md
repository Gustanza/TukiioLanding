## Origin story: TiME Tickets (2013–2015)

Source: *How We Made It in Africa*, "Start-up snapshot: Enabling Tanzanians to purchase event tickets with mobile money" (13 Feb 2015).

- **Co-founders:** Mwasapi Kihongosi and Godluck Akyoo.
- **Problem:** In Dar es Salaam you had to physically visit an outlet to buy event tickets.
- **Product:** An Android app where you **pay with mobile money**, receive a **QR-code ticket** on your phone, and get **scanned at the door**. It was used for events and cinemas.
- **Business model:** Commission on tickets. Target clients were event organisers, movie theatres and travel agents.
- **Milestones:** Pitched at **Pivot East 2013**. Won the **2013 Vodacom Appstar** challenge (TZS 1M ≈ US$550). Received a **US$15,000 grant** from the Tanzania–Finland Innovation Fund programme. Bootstrapped from other IT work, and turned down a $5,000 offer from partners whose direction they doubted.
- **Ambitions then:** Scanners plus marketing; expand to iOS and Windows; become a "one-stop ticketing solutions provider".
- **Quotes:** "Our goal is to make ticketing easy." / "The potential is big." (Kihongosi)

➡️ **Why it matters for the landing page:** Tukiio has a real **10+ year local story** (2013 → today). Mobile money and QR tickets were its first product, long before that was common. That story is a strong credibility asset that the current site doesn't tell.

## Parent company: Dephics

- Self-described as a "full-service creative and tech company" with "over a decade" of work.
- Services: mobile apps and software, websites, branding and creative design, hosting and domains, technical support, bulk printing, **barcoding as a service**, SEO, and WhatsApp chatbots. The printing and barcoding capabilities explain why Tukiio can offer cheap QR-coded paper tickets.
- Vision: "most preferred, innovative, and trusted brand" in creative and digital solutions across Africa by 2030.
- Products: **Tukiio** and **Hosting** (hosting.dephics.com).
- The website shows about 30 client logos ("For Over a Decade") without naming them.

## Business model (as stated)

1. **Free events are free, forever** (basic features).
2. **Paid events: 7% commission** on the ticket fee, said to cover the payment gateway and transfer handling. It is deducted at payout.
3. **Payouts within 24 hours** of the organiser's request.
4. **Custom packages** quoted per event (tools, services, hardware).
5. VC4A profile adds: **equipment and materials sales**, **white-label and institutional platform licensing**, and **emerging AI analytics subscriptions**. Customers: organisers, agencies, venues and institutions across Tanzania. VC4A pitch line: *"Digital infrastructure for events, ticketing, and civic participation."*
