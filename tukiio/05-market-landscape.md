# 05 — Market & Competitive Landscape

## Context: Tanzania

- **Mobile money is the default** way to pay: M-Pesa (Vodacom), Mixx by Yas (formerly Tigo Pesa), Airtel Money, HaloPesa, and the national interoperable **TanQR** standard. Cards are secondary.
- **Feature phones and patchy data** are still common outside the urban core, so USSD/SMS ticketing and paper tickets matter. The 2015 TiME Tickets article already called low smartphone penetration "a risk or an opportunity".
- **Swahili-first audiences**, with English used for business and conferences.
- Event venues that recur in Tukiio's catalogue: **JNICC**, **Hyatt Regency The Kilimanjaro**, **Serena**, **UDSM New Library**, **Super Dome**, **Coco Beach**, Alliance Française, Golden Tulip Zanzibar.
- Seasonality: an Oct–Dec peak (awards, festivals, year-end forums), with marathons through the year.

## Competitors & alternatives

| Player | Positioning | Notes vs Tukiio |
|---|---|---|
| **Otapp** (otapp.co.tz, OEvent app) | "Tanzania's premier electronic and online ticketing platform", covering **bus, flights, movies, events, theme parks** since 2014 | Broad consumer travel/tickets marketplace. Tukiio is deeper on **event management** (registration, check-in, engagement, voting, marathons, hardware). |
| **Zeraket** (zeraket.com) | "Africa's event technology and event management platform" covering ticketing, discovery and organiser tools; regional (e.g. Nyege Nyege 2026) | Pan-African, polished positioning. Tukiio's edge is local depth: USSD, paper, on-site services and Swahili. |
| **Ticket Kwanza** (Hosanna Higher Technologies, Dar) | Event e-ticketing and entry control, agent sales, stadium and social events | Older and narrower. |
| **Quicket × Ticketmaster** | Ticketmaster entered East Africa via Quicket (Kenya, Uganda) with M-Pesa/MTN/Airtel (Aug 2026 report); **Tanzania not confirmed** | A global entrant arriving next door is the threat. Tukiio should own the "local, complete, trusted" story now. |
| Manual / WhatsApp + printed tickets | The status quo for many organisers | Tukiio replaces this and still prints the paper tickets, at 500 TZS/pc. |
| Google Forms + bank/mobile-money transfers | Common for registrations and trainings | Tukiio's free tier for free events covers this. |

## Positioning statement (proposed)

> **For** Tanzanian event organisers, from churches and campuses to concerts, conferences and marathons,
> **Tukiio is** the all-in-one event platform
> **that** sells tickets everywhere people are (web, app, USSD, agents and paper), takes mobile money, verifies every entry, and pays out in 24 hours.
> **Unlike** generic ticket marketplaces, Tukiio runs the whole event: registration, check-in, live engagement, voting, wristbands and badges, backed by 10+ years of local experience.

## Audiences for the landing page

1. **Organisers (primary):** event agencies, promoters, corporates, NGOs, universities, associations, sports clubs, pageants and awards. Their jobs: sell more, avoid fraud, get paid fast, look professional, reduce queue chaos.
2. **Institutions (white-label):** government agencies, schools, banks, telcos. Their jobs: their own branded portal, reporting, compliance.
3. **Attendees (secondary):** find events, pay easily, get tickets safely. Serve them with a "Browse events" path and app download.
